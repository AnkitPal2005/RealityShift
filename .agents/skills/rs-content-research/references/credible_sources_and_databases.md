# Credible Scientific Databases & Source Verification Index

This document catalogs trusted databases, institutional archives, citation protocols, and evaluation standards for astrophysics, planetary science, AI architectures, quantum systems, and theoretical physics for RealityShift (@RealityShiftByAnkit).

---

## 1. Primary Astronomy & Space Exploration Databases

### 1.1 Institutional Space Agencies (Authoritative Background & Mission Data)
- **NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) & Horizons System**:
  - Definitive planetary ephemerides, NEO (Near Earth Object) trajectories, impact probabilities (Sentry Risk Table).
  - URL: [https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/)
- **NASA Exoplanet Archive**:
  - Confirmed exoplanets, stellar parameters, habitable zone calculations.
  - URL: [https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/)
- **ESA (European Space Agency) Science & Technology**:
  - Gaia stellar mapping database (1+ billion stars), James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) data releases.
  - URL: [https://www.cosmos.esa.int](https://www.cosmos.esa.int)
- **ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)**:
  - Chandrayaan-3 lunar surface data, Aditya-L1 solar observation updates, Gaganyaan mission architectures.
  - URL: [https://www.isro.gov.in](https://www.isro.gov.in)

### 1.2 Peer-Reviewed Astrophysics & Physics Journals (Primary Research)
- **Nature / Nature Astronomy**: Premier multidisciplinary peer-reviewed research.
- **Science / Science Advances**: Premier peer-reviewed research.
- **The Astrophysical Journal (ApJ) & ApJ Letters**: Standard peer-reviewed astronomy and astrophysics.
- **Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society (MNRAS)**: Detailed peer-reviewed planetary & galactic studies.
- **Icarus**: The premier journal of planetary science and orbital dynamics (e.g. Lissauer et al. 2012).
- **Physical Review Letters (PRL) & Physical Review D (PRD)**: Fundamental quantum mechanics, general relativity, and cosmological fields.

## 2. Artificial Intelligence, Computing & Quantum Science

### 2.1 Peer-Reviewed & Independent Research Standards
- Prioritize independent academic peer-reviewed venues:
  - **NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems)**
  - **ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations)**
  - **ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)**
  - **ACM / IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence**

### 2.2 Corporate Source Disclosure Protocol
When evaluating AI advancements, benchmarks, or safety claims originating from corporate labs (e.g., Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta AI, Microsoft Research):
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Explicitly note in the research brief: `[CORPORATE RESEARCH - LAB: <Company Name>]`.
- **Cross-Verification Requirement**: Corporate claims regarding benchmarks, parameter efficiencies, or frontier capabilities must be cross-referenced with independent academic evaluations (e.g. Stanford HAI AI Index, Epoch AI, Center for Human-Compatible AI) to prevent accepting corporate marketing or self-serving hype as objective scientific fact.

### 2.3 Quantum Computing & Information Theory
- **Physical Review Letters / Nature Physics**: Peer-reviewed quantum entanglement, topological qubits, and quantum algorithm complexity.
- **IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering**: Practical quantum hardware engineering and error correction thresholds.

## 3. Preprints (arXiv) Evaluation Protocol

While arXiv (`astro-ph`, `gr-qc`, `quant-ph`, `cs.AI`) is an invaluable repository for frontier physics and machine learning:
1. **Preliminary Status**: Every paper sourced solely from arXiv that has not yet completed peer review must be classified as: `[PREPRINT - PRELIMINARY FINDING]`.
2. **Verification Check**: Always search NASA ADS, Crossref, or Google Scholar to check whether the arXiv preprint has since been formally published in a peer-reviewed journal. If published, cite the final peer-reviewed journal and DOI.
3. **Never Build Core Conclusions Solely on Fringe Preprints**: High-stakes claims (e.g., "Warp drive invented", "Dark matter disproven", "AGI achieved") must never rely on a single unverified preprint.

## 4. Citation & Reference Logging Standards

Every cited reference in a RealityShift research brief must record:
1. **Authors**: Full last names and initials of leading authors (or institutional collaboration name).
2. **Year**: Year of publication.
3. **Title**: Full paper or report title.
4. **Journal / Publisher / Mission**: Publication venue (e.g., *Nature*, *Icarus*, *NASA Technical Report*).
5. **DOI or Stable URL**: Exact Digital Object Identifier (`https://doi.org/...`) or permanent institutional URL.
6. **Peer-Review Status**: Explicitly labeled as `[VERIFIED PEER-REVIEWED]`, `[VERIFIED INSTITUTIONAL]`, `[PREPRINT - PRELIMINARY]`, or `[CORPORATE DISCLOSED]`.
