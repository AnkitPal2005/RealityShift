# RealityShift Content Research: QA & Verification Checklist

Before any research brief generated by `rs-content-research` is finalized or handed over to the scriptwriter, it must satisfy 100% of the checks in this audit checklist.

---

## 1. Scientific Rigor & Classification Checks

- [ ] **1.1 Three-Tier Classification**: Are all claims strictly tagged as `[ESTABLISHED SCIENCE]`, `[THEORETICAL MODEL]`, or `[SPECULATIVE WHAT-IF]`?
- [ ] **1.2 Theoretical Zone vs. Numerical Extent of Variation**:
  - Does the report clearly distinguish between the theoretically accessible chaotic zone ($0^\circ - 85^\circ$, Laskar et al. 1993) and the modeled extent of variation ($\approx 20^\circ - 25^\circ$ in extent, Lissauer et al. 2012)?
  - Is an $85^\circ$ tilt strictly presented as a non-inevitable, low-probability outcome over ultra-deep time rather than an immediate disaster?
  - Has the researcher avoided converting an extent of variation into an arbitrary absolute angular range?
- [ ] **1.3 Timescale Separation**: Are immediate real-time physical effects ($0\text{s} - 24\text{h}$) strictly separated from long-term secular/chaotic orbital evolution ($10^6 - 10^9\text{ years}$)?
- [ ] **1.4 Barycenter Reference vs. Physical Displacement**: Is the Earth-Moon barycenter ($\approx 4,670\text{ km}$ from Earth's center) treated correctly as a system center-of-mass reference point rather than claiming Earth physically jumps or collapses by $4,670\text{ km}$?
- [ ] **1.5 Model-Dependent Climate Consequences**: Are climate and atmospheric impacts explicitly labeled as model-dependent (GCMs) rather than deterministic certainties?
- [ ] **1.6 Myth Elimination & Tidal Nuances**:
  - Has a structured myth-busting table debunked sensationalist viral tropes (e.g. tides dropping to zero, instant mega-tsunamis, instantaneous planetary flipping)?
  - Does the tidal analysis retain the equilibrium forcing ratio ($\approx 0.46$) while explicitly acknowledging that coastal tidal ranges do NOT uniformly decrease by $54\%$ due to amphidromic systems and ocean basin resonance?

---

## 2. Physics Calculations & Math Auditing

- [ ] **2.1 Explicit Units**: Are all physical quantities accompanied by SI units and relatable vernacular equivalents?
- [ ] **2.2 Formula Applicability Checked**: Did the researcher verify that boundary conditions and assumptions (e.g. point-mass vs distributed mass, fluid vs rigid Roche limits) apply to the scenario?
- [ ] **2.3 Light-Travel vs Gravitational Radiation**: Is $d/c$ accurately identified as the light-travel time from the Moon's distance rather than mistakenly called a "gravitational wave delay"? Is instantaneous mass disappearance noted as an unphysical hypothetical premise?
- [ ] **2.4 Stated Assumptions & Inputs**: Are all physical constants, masses, distances, and assumptions explicitly listed prior to calculation?
- [ ] **2.5 Step-by-Step Derivations**: Are intermediate mathematical steps clearly written out for auditability?
- [ ] **2.6 Analytical vs. Simulation Distinction**: Are simplified order-of-magnitude estimates clearly distinguished from complex N-body or hydrodynamic numerical simulations?

---

## 3. Source Quality & Traceability

- [ ] **3.1 Peer-Reviewed Research Priority**: Are core scientific claims anchored in peer-reviewed journals (*Nature*, *Science*, *ApJ*, *Icarus*, *Physical Review*)?
- [ ] **3.2 Institutional Authority**: Are baseline astronomical values and mission facts verified through institutional databases (NASA JPL Horizons, ESA, ISRO, USGS, NOAA)?
- [ ] **3.3 Preprint (arXiv) Treatment**: Are arXiv preprints treated as preliminary findings unless verified to have passed formal peer review?
- [ ] **3.4 Corporate AI Disclosure**: If covering AI technologies or benchmarks, is corporate research explicitly tagged (`[CORPORATE RESEARCH - LAB: <Name>]`) and cross-checked against independent academic research?
- [ ] **3.5 Complete Metadata**: Does every cited source record authors, publication year, journal/institution, and DOI or stable URL?

---

## 4. Visual & Scriptwriter Preparedness

- [ ] **4.1 AI Prompt Compatibility**: Are the 5 visual scenes composed with concrete optical, atmospheric, and lighting descriptors suitable for Midjourney / Flux / Imagen prompts?
- [ ] **4.2 Vertical 9:16 Framing**: Are focal subjects and perspective designed specifically for a 9:16 mobile canvas?
- [ ] **4.3 Conversational Hindi Pacing**: Are the 3 opening hooks and 5 title candidates written in natural, high-retention conversational Hindi without robotic TTS loanword traps?
- [ ] **4.4 Gate Enforcement**: Is Section 11 marked `PENDING HUMAN APPROVAL`, explicitly halting work before `rs-script-writer` begins?
