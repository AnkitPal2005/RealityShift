## 3. Explicit Physics Calculations & Analytical Checks

### 3.1 Light-Travel Propagation Time
- **Physical Property**: Propagation delay of changes in gravitational and electromagnetic fields across the Earth-Moon distance.
- **Governing Formula**: $\Delta t = \frac{d_M}{c}$
- **Applicability Check**: In General Relativity, local changes in gravitational fields propagate at the speed of light in vacuum ($c$).
- **Hypothetical Premise Caveat**: Instantaneous disappearance of mass violates local conservation of stress-energy ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$). In this thought experiment, the disappearance is treated purely as an idealized conceptual trigger. It does **not** generate a standard gravitational wave (which requires a time-varying quadrupole moment $\ddot{I}_{jk}$); rather, it marks the time after which Earth's spacetime metric transitions away from the Moon's static gravitational influence.
- **Input Values**:
  - $d_M = 3.844 \times 10^8\text{ m}$
  - $c = 2.99792 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$
- **Calculation**:
  $$\Delta t = \frac{3.844 \times 10^8\text{ m}}{2.99792 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}} \approx 1.282\text{ seconds}$$
- **Result**: Earth continues its orbit around the mutual Earth-Moon barycenter for $1.28\text{ seconds}$ before the absence of lunar gravity affects Earth.

### 3.2 Solar vs. Lunar Equilibrium Tidal Acceleration Ratio
- **Governing Formula**:
  $$\frac{a_{\text{tidal, Sun}}}{a_{\text{tidal, Moon}}} = \frac{M_\odot / d_\odot^3}{M_M / d_M^3} = \frac{M_\odot}{M_M} \left( \frac{d_M}{d_\odot} \right)^3$$
- **Assumptions**: Spherical bodies, equilibrium tide approximation on an idealized ocean-covered sphere without continental barriers or basin resonance.
- **Input Values**:
  - $M_\odot = 1.989 \times 10^{30}\text{ kg}$
  - $M_M = 7.342 \times 10^{22}\text{ kg}$
  - $d_M = 3.844 \times 10^8\text{ m}$
  - $d_\odot = 1.496 \times 10^{11}\text{ m}$
- **Step-by-Step Derivation**:
  $$\frac{M_\odot}{M_M} = \frac{1.989 \times 10^{30}}{7.342 \times 10^{22}} \approx 2.709 \times 10^7$$
  $$\frac{d_M}{d_\odot} = \frac{3.844 \times 10^8}{1.496 \times 10^{11}} \approx 2.570 \times 10^{-3}$$
  $$\left( \frac{d_M}{d_\odot} \right)^3 \approx (2.570 \times 10^{-3})^3 \approx 1.697 \times 10^{-8}$$
  $$\text{Ratio} \approx (2.709 \times 10^7) \times (1.697 \times 10^{-8}) \approx 0.460$$
- **Analytical Ratio vs. Dynamic Reality**:
  - *Analytical Equilibrium Ratio*: The Sun exerts approximately $46\%$ of the Moon's equilibrium tidal forcing.
  - *Dynamic Ocean Basin Nuance*: Actual coastal tides are **not** simple equilibrium tides. They are dynamic amphidromic systems governed by ocean bathymetry, coastal geometry, local resonance, and the Coriolis effect. Therefore, one **cannot** claim every coastal tidal range will decrease by exactly $54\%$. Rather, the lunar tidal component (M2 tide) drops to zero, and the remaining tide is driven solely by the solar component (S2 tide), drastically altering amphidromic points, tidal timing, and local amplitude depending on geographic location.

### 5.4 Deep-Time Secular & Chaotic Obliquity Evolution ($10^6 - 10^9\text{ years}$) — `[THEORETICAL MODEL]`
- **Laskar, Joutel, & Robutel (1993, *Nature*)**:
