## 1. Creative Vision & High-Concept Logline
- **Visual Logline**: A visually arresting, scientifically honest thought experiment that contrasts the sudden, eerie silence of a moonless night sky with the colossal, slow-motion destabilization of Earth's planetary climate over millions of years.
- **Core Aesthetic Thesis**: Reject sensationalist Hollywood disaster CGI (no 500m mega-tsunamis, no cartoon planet explosions). Instead, evoke deep existential awe by capturing authentic NASA-grade planetary photorealism, eerie starlit coastal twilights, and delicate orbital mechanics.
- **Visual Pacing Dynamic**: Starts with cold, quiet celestial observation $\to$ sudden sensory plunge into pitch-dark urban shores $\to$ grounded, calm marine readjustment $\to$ sweeping, awe-inspiring orbital visualization of secular obliquity variation $\to$ serene, fragile closing contemplation.

---

## 2. Overall Visual Treatment
- **Cinematography Format**: 9:16 Vertical ($1080 \times 1920$), 24fps filmic shutter (180° equivalent).
- **Optics & Lens Simulation**:
  - Space shots: 65mm IMAX format, Hasselblad archival space photography aesthetic, ultra-clean contrast, razor-sharp crater rims.
  - Terrestrial coastal shots: 35mm anamorphic prime lens, subtle edge softness, natural vertical flare streaks from city lights.
- **Film Grain & Texture**: 35mm fine grain (ISO 250), organic highlight rolloff on planetary limbs, zero plastic digital noise.
- **Lighting Philosophy**: Uncompromising physical truth. In deep space, sunlight is brutal, single-source, and directional with zero atmospheric diffusion. On Earth, twilight transitions to starlight-only darkness ($0.001\text{ lux}$).
- **Safe Zone Architecture**:
  - Primary Focal Zone: $y = 380 - 1150$ (central visual area).
  - HUD Ribbon Band: $y = 280 - 380$ (centered at $x = 540$).
  - Caption Band: Existing `lego.py` safe zone is $y = 1150 - 1250$; proposed expanded target for kinetic captions is $y = 1150 - 1320$.
  - Bottom Clear Margin: Existing `lego.py` clear band is $y > 1520$; proposed conservative mobile margin is $y > 1450$.

## 14. Scientific Accuracy & Epistemological Warnings

- **`[ESTABLISHED SCIENCE]` Protection**:
  - *Tsunami Myth Banned*: Shot S03-A and S04-A explicitly depict **calm, receding water**, completely prohibiting any giant breaking tidal waves or drowning cities.
  - *Ocean Response Nuance*: The removal of lunar tidal forcing does **not** mean all coastlines instantly drain. Water sloshes dynamically through ocean basins over hours and days; solar tidal forcing remains. Shot S04-A is an `[ILLUSTRATIVE VIGNETTE]` showing a local low-tide setting to visually communicate altered tidal schedules, not a claim of instantaneous global ocean drainage.
  - *Zero Barycenter Physical Lurch*: Shot S01-A and S02-A depict Earth continuing smoothly along its orbital path; Earth does **not** shake, crack, or lurch in space.
- **`[THEORETICAL MODEL]` Framing**:
  - *Obliquity vs. Precession (Shot S05-A)*: The Lissauer et al. (2012) finding of $\approx 20^\circ - 25^\circ$ represents the **modeled extent of obliquity (axial tilt) variation** ($\Delta \varepsilon \sim 20^\circ - 25^\circ$) over hundreds of millions of years. It must **not** be described as precession, nor presented as an absolute tilt angle, and must **not** depict an immediate 85° flip. It is visually framed as a theoretical simulation overlay (`// THEORETICAL MODEL: SIMULATED OBLIQUITY VARIATION EXTENT`).
- **Scale Compression Disclosure**:
  - *Notice for Shot S01-A & S02-A*: `[ARTISTIC SCALE COMPRESSION: The Earth-Moon orbital separation is compressed to allow both bodies to be visible in a single vertical 9:16 frame for narrative clarity]`.
